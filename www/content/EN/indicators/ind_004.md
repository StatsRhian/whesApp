## Indicator information

The data was acquired from the [Labour Force Survey (LFS) via Health & Safety Executive (HSE)](https://www.hse.gov.uk/statistics/lfs/index.htm#illness). The data is taken from Table 1 of Country and region of residence dataset ,`LFSILLREG.xlsx`. Data is for a three-year average covering the period 2016/17-2018/19.
