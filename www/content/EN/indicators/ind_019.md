## Indicator information

The data was acquired from the [National Survey for Wales, 2017-2018](https://beta.ukdataservice.ac.uk/datacatalogue/studies/study?id=8390). The data was extracted from the variable `DvBedroom`. Sample weighting from the column  `SampleAdultWeight` were applied to make the data more representative of the general population and to account for sub-sampling.  Age, gender and education breakdowns are not available for this variable, as the question relates to the household. Confidence intervals were calculated using the `binom.wilson()` function from the [{binom}](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/binom/binom.pdf) R package.
