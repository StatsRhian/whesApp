## Indicator information

The data was acquired from the [National Survey for Wales, 2018-2019](https://beta.ukdataservice.ac.uk/datacatalogue/studies/study?id=8591). The data was extracted from the variable `GenHealth`. Confidence intervals were calculated using the `binom.wilson()` function from the  [{binom}](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/binom/binom.pdf) R package.

Surveyed people were asked to report on their highest level of education. The derived responses were original stored as variable `DvHiQual2`. The original response options were:

1. National Qualification Framework levels 4-8
2. National Qualification Framework level 3
3. National Qualification Framework level 2
4. Below National Qualification Framework level 2
5. No qualification

These options were re-coded to form a four category variable `Education` as follows  High = 1, Medium = 2, Low = 3 & 4, None = 5.
