## Indicator information

The data was acquired from [StatsWales](https://statswales.gov.wales/Catalogue/Community-Safety-and-Social-Inclusion/Poverty/peopleinrelativeincomepoverty-by-whetherthereisdisabilitywithinthefamily) via their API. The data is for 2016-17 to 2018-19. The measure reported is the risk of being in relative income poverty, for people with the given characteristic (%)
