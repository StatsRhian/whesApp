## About this project

The overall aim of the WHESRi is to inform, enable and support policy solutions,innovation and sustainable investment towards healthy, prosperous lives for all,
increasing equity in health and leaving no one behind in Wales and beyond. The project supports the Building A Healthier Wales and the Welsh Government Transformation
Programme, facilitating, monitoring and accelerating joint action across sectors,governance levels and key stakeholders to help achieve a Healthier, a More Equal, a
Prosperous and a Globally Responsible Wales for the present and future generations.

To accomplish this, the project has the following overarching objectives:

i. Gather and analyse data and policies, providing a benchmark dynamic picture of
health inequities, their determinants, economic burden and relevant existing
policy interventions in Wales;

ii. Develop an interactive digital platform and information gateway to host the
Welsh health equity dataset and related WHESRi products, tools and resources;

iii. To monitor, review, explore, assess, model and forecast the effect of policy
changes and investment decisions on wellbeing and health equity; and

iv. To facilitate cross-sector whole-of-government, whole-of-society policy dialogue,
commitment and action, embedding and progressing health and equity, as part
of the sustainable development agenda.
