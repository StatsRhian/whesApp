## Health and social and human capital

#### [Together for Mental Health Delivery Plan 2019-2022](https://gov.wales/mental-health-delivery-plan-2019-to-2022)

*POLICY AND STRATEGY* (**COVID**)

The Welsh Government plan to improve the mental health of the nation.

#### [Youth Engagement and Progression Framework](https://gov.wales/youth-engagement-and-progression-framework-implementation-plan)

*POLICY AND STRATEGY*

The Welsh Government implementation plan to reduce the number of 11 to 25 year olds who are not engaged in education, employment or training (NEET).

#### [Reducing Infant Class Sizes Grant](https://gov.wales/reducing-infant-class-sizes-grant-progress-report-2017-2019)

*POLICY AND STRATEGY*

The Welsh Government reports sets out information on the progress it is making to reduce infant class sizes in grant-funded schools.

#### [Supporting young people in the Justice system](https://gov.wales/supporting-young-people-justice-system)

*POLICY AND STRATEGY*

The Welsh Government Youth Justice Implementation Plan to support young people in or at risk of entering the criminal justice system.

#### [Healthy Weight: Healthy Wales obesity strategy](https://gov.wales/health-weight-healthy-wales-obesity-strategy)

*COLLECTION*

The Welsh Government strategy sets out what is doing to support people to make healthier choices and lead more active lives, including healthy childcare settings, schools, and higher, further and other educational settings.

#### [Equality plan and objectives: 2020 to 2024](https://gov.wales/equality-plan-and-objectives-2020-2024)

*POLICY AND STRATEGY*

The Welsh Government plan sets out aims, objectives and actions  to tackle inequality and make our society fairer and more inclusive, including the long-term aim that everyone in Wales is able to participate in political, public and everyday life.

#### [Coronavirus reconstruction: challenges and priorities](https://gov.wales/coronavirus-reconstruction-challenges-and-priorities)

*POLICY AND STRATEGY*

The Welsh Government document sets out priorities to help stabilise Wales as we continue to live with coronavirus, prevent longer-term damage and plan to build a new future, including ensuring that young people do not lose educationally or economically.

#### [Digital strategy for Wales](https://gov.wales/digital-strategy-wales)

*POLICY AND STRATEGY*

The Welsh Government strategy on using digital, data and technology to improve the lives of people in Wales, including providing the people of Wales with the confidence they need to engage in their communities and in modern society.

#### [Programme for government 2021 to 2026](https://gov.wales/programme-for-government-2021-to-2026)

*POLICY AND STRATEGY*

The updated Welsh Government programme for government, including a commitent to continue its long-term programme of education reform, and ensure educational inequalities narrow and standards rise.

#### [Cymraeg 2050: Our plan for 2021 - 2026](https://gov.wales/cymraeg-2050-our-plan-2021-2026)

*POLICY AND STRATEGY*

The Welsh Government work programme 2021 to 2026 to help achieve a million Welsh speakers and double the daily use of Welsh by 2050.
