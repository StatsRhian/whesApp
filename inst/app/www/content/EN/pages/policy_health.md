## Policies and legislation affecting health and health services

<h4><a href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/anaw/2014/4/contents" target="_blank">Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014</a></h4>

*LEGISLATION*

An Act of the National Assembly for Wales providing the legal framework for improving the well-being of people who need care and support, and carers who need support, and for transforming social services in Wales.

<h4><a href="https://gov.wales/coronavirus-act-2020-commencement-no-1-wales-regulations-2020" target="_blank">Coronavirus Act 2020 (Commencement No.1) (Wales) Regulations 2020</a></h4>

*LEGISLATION* (**COVID**)

The Regulations bring into force Coronavirus Act 2020 provisions relating to the Mental Health Review Tribunal for Wales and local authorities' duties under the Social Services and Well-being (Wales) Act 2014.

<h4><a href="https://gov.wales/substance-misuse-delivery-plan-2019-2022-0" target="_blank">Substance Misuse Delivery Plan 2019-2022</a></h4>

*POLICY AND STRATEGY* (**COVID**)

The Welsh Government delivery plan aims to reduce the harms associated with substance misuse during and following the COVID-19 pandemic.

<h4><a href="https://gov.wales/sites/default/files/publications/2019-10/a-healthier-wales-action-plan.pdf" target="_blank">A Healthier Wales: Our plan for health and social care</a></h4>

*POLICY AND STRATEGY*

The Welsh Government plan sets out a long term future vision of a ‘whole system approach to  health and social care’, focused on health and wellbeing and on  preventing illness.

<h4><a href="Healthy Weight: Healthy Wales obesity strategy" target="_blank">Healthy Weight: Healthy Wales obesity strategy</a></h4>

*COLLECTION*
