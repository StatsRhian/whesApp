## Indicator information

The data was acquired from [StatsWales](https://statswales.gov.wales/Catalogue/Health-and-Social-Care/NHS-Hospital-Activity/NHS-Beds/nhsbedssummarydata-by-year). The data is from 2018-19. The measure reported is average daily available beds. The number of available beds per 100,000 inhabitants was calculated assuming the population of Wales as 3,152,879. This was based on the 2019 mid-year population [estimates from ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/p
opulationestimates/datasets/lowersuperoutputareamidyearpopulationestimates).
