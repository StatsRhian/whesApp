## Indicator information

The data was acquired from the [Infant mortality statistics, Office for National Statistics](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/birthsdeathsandmarriages/deaths/datasets/infantmortalitybirthcohorttablesinenglandandwales). The data is taken from Table 3 of the 2017 edition of this data set. Infant deaths are given per 1,000 live births.
