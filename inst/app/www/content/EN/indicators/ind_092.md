## Indicator information

The data was acquired from the [Cervical  Screening Wales Annual Statistical Report 2018-19](http://www.cervicalscreeningwales.wales.nhs.uk/sitesplus/documents/1032/Cervical%20Screening%20Wales%20Annual%20Statistical%20Report%202018-19.V1.0.pdf). Data is for  1 April 2018 to 31 March 2019. Note: The eligible age range for cervical screening is 25-64 years. Individuals over 50 are routinely invited for screening every 5 years, coverage within 3.5 years is not applicable in this age group.
